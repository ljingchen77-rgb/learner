Department of Engineering Physics, Tsinghua University · Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Yi Tan

Feasibility Study

Research question: Can a tokamak serve as an industrial 13.5 nm EUV lithography source?

Application boundary
Identified radiative collapse, narrow operating windows, and optical-system incompatibilities.

Read research details

Method: Systematic literature review, order-of-magnitude estimation, and qualitative reasoning across atomic physics, impurity transport, power balance, and operational stability.

Finding: The optimal 30—50 eV radiation window of Sn partially overlaps with tokamak divertor conditions, while density, emitting volume, time structure, and radiance remain substantially mismatched.

Conclusion: Tokamaks are unlikely to become industrial EUV lithography sources under foreseeable conditions, but remain valuable platforms for high-Z impurity radiation and edge-transport studies.
